Reproducibility
================

code/reproduce.sh produces every figure and table, one stage at a time,
in this order:

  ./fetch_data.sh                 # from the repository root: trained networks,
                                  # migrate-n output and score tables from Zenodo
                                  # (FETCH_LARGE=1 adds the chromosome
                                  # genealogies, 5.9 GB)
  cd code
  ./reproduce.sh simulate        # simulated data sets of the first networks (seeded)
  ./reproduce.sh train            # first networks; TRAIN_JOBS=8 TRAIN_THREADS=2 by default
  ./reproduce.sh calibration
  ./reproduce.sh recovery
  MIGRATE_NP=16 ./reproduce.sh adh
  ./reproduce.sh revision-simulate   # log-scaled features, event ages, nested design
  ./reproduce.sh revision-train      # main networks, 10 training runs; weight selection; nested design
  ./reproduce.sh revision            # penalty comparison, MLE, checks; calls revision-featlog
  ./reproduce.sh chrom-simulate      # n=100 training sets
  ./reproduce.sh chrom-train
  ./reproduce.sh chrom               # needs data/chrom-n100 (see below)
  ./reproduce.sh growth-scan
  DGRP_TAR=... MIGRATE_NP=16 ./reproduce.sh adh-flanks
  ./reproduce.sh adh-runs            # needs the score tables of all 10 training runs (see below)

"./reproduce.sh all" runs only the stages from simulate to revision; the
chromosome and Adh stages after it need migrate-n runs on a cluster.
With the files from fetch_data.sh, the training and migrate-n steps can be
skipped: the networks, the migrate-n output and the score tables are in
place, and each stage only rebuilds its figures and tables.

Outputs go to figures/. Tables are written as LaTeX fragments
(figures/tab_*.tex); each script's printed output is kept in
figures/logs/<script>.txt.

The folder tutorial/ walks through the core of the pipeline (simulate,
features, training, test) for one regime; see tutorial/tutorial.pdf.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
migrate-n, Adh            inputs/adh/run_migrate.slurm: the 15 four-gamete
                          segments, 1000 posterior genealogies per segment
                          (same as MIGRATE_NP=16 ./reproduce.sh adh)
Adh neutral calibration   inputs/adh/adh_neutral_cal.slurm (array 1-50):
                          adh_neutral_sim.py -> four-gamete segments ->
                          migrate-n -> data/adh-neutral-cal/rep<i>/
Adh scores, 10 runs       inputs/adh/adh_rescore_seeds.slurm (array 1-52,
                          REAL_DIR = the Adh migrate-n directory): scores the
                          calibration replicates, the 15 Adh segments and Adh
                          with its flanks with the n=176 networks of training
                          runs 67-76
Simulated chromosomes     inputs/chrom/chrom_pipeline.slurm with NSAMP=100,
                          WINDOW_BP=10000, OUTDIR=data/chrom-n100 and
                          KIND = sweep, sweep-s20, sweep-s50, neutral
                          (constant prior) and sweep, neutral (growth prior),
                          100 replicates each: msprime -> migrate-n -> scores
                          of training run 67
Chromosome scores         inputs/chrom/rescore_seeds.slurm: scores of
                          training runs 68-76

Inputs
------
code/inputs/adh/dgrp_adh_205lines.fasta   Adh region of the 205 DGRP lines as released by the
                                          Drosophila Genome Nexus (Lack et al. 2015; docs/adh-data.md);
                                          29 lines without data at Adh are dropped, leaving 176
code/inputs/adh/parmfile                  migrate-n settings for the Adh posterior sample (also used
                                          for the flanks and the neutral calibration, with their own
                                          random seeds)
code/inputs/chrom/parmfile[-growth]       migrate-n settings for the simulated chromosomes
DGRP_TAR                                  dgrp_Chr2L.tar of the Drosophila Genome Nexus, for the
                                          10 kb flanks of Adh (docs/adh-data.md)
The McDonald-Kreitman outgroup sequences (D. simulans, D. yakuba) are
fetched from NCBI by accession in mcdonald_kreitman_test.py.

Determinism
-----------
Simulations are seeded (simulate_sweep.py passes a per-draw seed to
simtree.py; msprime runs and migrate-n runs take explicit seeds), and
every network has a fixed training seed (training runs 67-76). With the
trained networks and score tables from Zenodo (fetch_data.sh), a rebuild
reproduces every table exactly. Retraining is bit-identical only on the
same hardware and software (the networks were trained on an Apple M4 Max,
PyTorch 2.12.1, 2 threads per job); on another CPU or PyTorch version each
retrained network behaves like a new training run.
migrate-n runs were made with version 6.1.11.
