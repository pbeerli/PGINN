Reproducibility
================

Every figure and table in the paper is produced by code/reproduce.sh:

  cd code
  ./reproduce.sh all          # or one stage at a time, in this order:
  ./reproduce.sh simulate     # simulated datasets (seeded)
  ./reproduce.sh train        # all networks; TRAIN_JOBS=8 TRAIN_THREADS=2 by default
  ./reproduce.sh calibration
  ./reproduce.sh recovery
  ./reproduce.sh detection
  ./reproduce.sh msprime
  MIGRATE_NP=16 ./reproduce.sh adh

Tables are written as LaTeX fragments (figures/tab_*.tex) that the paper
includes directly, so the typeset numbers are the scripts' output. Numbers
quoted in the running text are printed by the scripts named below; each
script's output is kept in figures/logs/<script>.txt.

Inputs
------
code/inputs/adh/dgrp_adh_205lines.fasta   Adh region of the 205 DGRP Freeze 2.0 lines (Huang et al. 2014);
                                          29 lines without data at Adh are dropped, leaving 176
code/inputs/adh/parmfile                  migrate-n settings for the Adh posterior sample
code/inputs/adh/run_migrate.slurm         the same run as a SLURM job (16 MPI ranks)
The McDonald-Kreitman outgroup sequences (D. simulans, D. yakuba) are
fetched from NCBI by accession in mcdonald_kreitman_test.py.

Determinism
-----------
Simulations are seeded (simulate_sweep.py passes a per-draw seed to
simtree.py; msprime runs take explicit seeds), and every network has a
fixed training seed. Neural-network training on a different CPU, thread
count or PyTorch version can still change the last digits of the results.
